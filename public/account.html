Setting it up on the Xteink (CrossPoint)
  1. Make a reader key here.
  2. On the reader, open the OPDS browser's settings and add a server.
  3. Enter the catalog address, your username, and the reader key as the password.
  4. Open the server: Newest lists everything; choose an item to download it.
